Keep life in order

Extras on this page are off until you turn them on in Settings.

Files

A private place for IDs, insurance, receipts, warranties and manuals. It isn’t a cloud drive, and it has an encrypted file of its own.

  • Bring files in

    Drag them onto the Mac window, add them on the iPhone, or scan a paper document with the camera.

  • Keep them in order

    Folders, a kind and dates for each document, and search by name or by the words in a PDF.

  • Know what expires

    Turn on expiry reminders. The alert only says that a document expires soon.

  • Take them with you

    Save one out anywhere, or copy them all out with Export Everything.

  • Habits

    A habit repeats every day, on certain days, or a number of times a week. Tap its circle to mark today.

    • Plain counts. “3 of 4 this week” and a twelve-week grid. No streaks, no rewards.
    • A reminder, if you want one. Pick a time, and it only rings on the days the habit is due.

  • Home and car maintenance logs

    Make a log for your car, house or furnace. Each entry holds the date, what was done, the cost and, for a car, the miles.

    • What’s due next. Add an item like an oil change every 6 months or 5,000 miles, and Stonefold works out when it’s due.
    • A reminder, if you want one. Due items can write their own reminder, so they show up in Today.
    • What it cost. See what a log has cost this year and in all.
