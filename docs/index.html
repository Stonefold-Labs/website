Apps that do one everyday job, plainly.

Stonefold Labs makes apps for iPhone and Mac. Each one does a single job you already understand: a budget, a note, a list, a date.

What you put in them is yours. There is no account to make, nothing is tracked, and you pay once.

What we’re making

  • Bellwether Budget

    In development

    A household budget you keep by hand, for iPhone and Mac. You type what you spend, and it tells you in plain words where you stand. No bank login. It never connects to your bank.

  • Small Flock

    In development

    A calm game. You are the border collie, gathering a small flock into the stone fold. No clock, no score, no way to lose.

Apps for notes, reminders and a calendar are being built too.
