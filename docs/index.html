They work together

Two switches in Settings let the parts share. Both start off.

  • Bills on the calendar

    Each bill shows on its due day, with its amount.

  • Bills in Reminders

    Waiting bills sit at the top of Today. Choose Paid and it’s recorded in the budget.

  • Reminders on the calendar

    A reminder with a day shows under that day’s events.

Encryption and privacy

Everything you keep is encrypted on your devices. We run no server for the app, so we can’t see what’s in it.

  • One unlock

    Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode opens the app. By default, anyone who can unlock your device can open it. You can lock the budget or the notes with a passphrase of your own.

  • Sync only if you choose it

    Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on iCloud sync. Then each change is sealed first, and only your devices hold the key.

  • The recovery sheet

    A page you print is the way back in if a device’s keys are lost. If you lose every device and the sheet, nobody can open what you kept, including us.
