How it works

Stonefold lives on your iPhone and Mac.

Your things stay on your iPhone and Mac, encrypted, and the app works without the internet. You enter everything yourself, and there’s no AI. If you want your iPhone and Mac to match, you can turn on iCloud sync.

  • Offline
  • Encrypted
  • Sync
  • Export
  • No account

1It works without the internet.

Everything you add is saved on your device first. There’s nothing to sign in to and nothing to wait for.

  • Turn on airplane mode and nothing changes.
  • Alerts are set on your device and ring from it.
  • Only iCloud sync uses the internet, and only if you turn it on.

2Everything is encrypted on your device.

Your things are stored in encrypted files, and the keys stay on your iPhone and Mac. We run no server for the app, so we can’t see what’s in it.

  • Keys stay on your iPhone and Mac

    Each part has its own file and its own random key. Each key is made on your device and kept there. We never see one.

  • One unlock

    Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode opens the app. By default, anyone who can unlock your device can open it. You can lock the budget or the notes with a passphrase of your own.

  • The recovery sheet

    A page you print, with a code for each file, is the way back in if a device’s keys are lost. If you lose your iPhone and Mac and the sheet, nobody can open what you kept, including us.

3Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on iCloud sync.

Out of the box, nothing leaves your device. Sync is a switch in Settings, and it starts off.

Turn it on and each change is sealed on your device first, with a key only your iPhone and Mac hold. It then travels through your own iCloud to your other iPhone or Mac. Sync is end-to-end encrypted. Only your iPhone and Mac hold the key. It runs through your own iCloud, and we run no servers.

  • Sync covers every section you keep, Files included: the Calendar, Reminders, Notes, Budget, Clock and Files.
  • A new device gets the key from one you already use, with a code you scan or type.
  • Apple can see that your Apple Account is syncing, and when, because sync runs through iCloud.

If you lose your iPhone and Mac and every copy of the key, nobody can open what you kept, including us. Print the recovery sheet and keep it where you would keep a key.

Stays on your device

  • What you keep, in encrypted files
  • The keys that open them
  • Your settings
  • Your alerts, which ring from your device

Leaves only because you chose it

  • Sealed changes, through your own iCloud, if you turn on sync
  • An export, in a folder you choose
  • A saved copy, sealed with a passphrase you set
  • The recovery sheet, a page you print

4You can export what you keep.

Your events, reminders, notes and budget can be exported in formats other apps open. Export Everything, in Settings, writes them into one new folder.

  • Calendar.ics for calendar apps
  • Reminders.csv for spreadsheet apps
  • Notes, one plain text file for each note
  • Budget.xlsx, your budget as a workbook
  • Files, your documents as they are

Nothing in the folder is encrypted, so Stonefold asks first. Choosing a folder in iCloud Drive uploads it there too.

Stonefold also keeps encrypted backups on your device, and Save a Copy seals a copy with a passphrase you choose.

5No account, no ads, no tracking, no AI.

  • No account

    No sign-up, and nothing to log in to.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No ads, no analytics, no outside services. We can’t see what’s in the app.

  • No AI

    Anything Stonefold works out is plain arithmetic, and it can show how.

  • You enter it yourself

    You type everything in. Nothing is imported or filled in on its own, and it connects to no bank.

  • Pay once

    Stonefold costs $24.99, once, for iPhone and Mac. No subscription, no in-app purchases.

Questions

Can you see what I keep?

No. We run no server for the app, so we never receive it.

Do I have to use sync?

No. It’s off to start, and everything works without it.

What if I lose my iPhone?

If you use sync, your other iPhone or Mac still holds what you kept. Either way, your recovery sheet or a saved copy opens it on a new device. If you lose your iPhone and Mac and every copy of the key, nobody can open it, including us.

What if I stop using Stonefold?

Export Everything puts your events, reminders, notes and budget in one folder, in formats other apps open.
