One price, paid once.

Stonefold costs $24.99, once, for iPhone and Mac together, and every future update is included.

What $24.99 pays for

  • Both devices

    One payment of $24.99 covers Stonefold on your iPhone and on your Mac.

  • Every part

    Calendar, Reminders, Notes, Budget, Tools and Files are all included, so nothing is held back for later.

  • Parts you choose

    You turn on the parts you want, and you can change that any time in Settings.

  • All future updates

    When we improve Stonefold, you get the improvements without paying again.

  • iCloud sync

    Sync is included and starts off. If you turn it on, it is end-to-end encrypted and runs through your own iCloud.

  • Export Everything

    Whenever you want, it writes what you keep into one folder, in formats other apps can open.

  • Themes and typefaces

    Nine themes and four typefaces let you make the app look and read the way you like.

  • Help and support

    Stonefold has its own Help, the website has a full guide, and you can write to support@stonefoldlabs.com. We answer within two working days.

What it never costs

  • No subscription

    There is nothing to renew and there are no in-app purchases, so $24.99 is the whole price.

  • No ads

    There are no ads in Stonefold.

  • No account

    You never have to sign up for anything to use the app.

  • No data sold

    We run no server for the app, so we have nothing of yours to sell.
