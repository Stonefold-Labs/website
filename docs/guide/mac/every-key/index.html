Guide / On the Mac

Every keyboard shortcut

Every key on the Mac, by section, in one place.

These are the keys the Mac app starts with, as Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts (⌘/) lists them: first the app’s own, then each section’s. Hold ⌘ for a moment and the app shows the keys for what is on screen. A key that needs a feature says which one. Only the sections you keep have keys.

A key works in its section while that section is on screen, so ⌘N is “new” in whichever one you are in. With Change Keys on in Settings, under Look, you can give any command in a section a key of your own. See Using the keyboard.

Going places

G and the letter after it work wherever you aren’t typing, and only for the sections you keep.

⌘1…⌘6Go to a section, in the order shown. The last number is the number of sections you keep.
⌃⌘TShow Today, across the sections. It is there once you keep two or more.
G TGo to Today (two or more sections kept)
G CGo to the Calendar
G RGo to the Reminders
G NGo to the Notes
G BGo to the Budget
G KGo to the Clock
G FGo to Files
G SGo to Settings
G HGo to Help
Hold ⌘Show the keys for what is on screen
