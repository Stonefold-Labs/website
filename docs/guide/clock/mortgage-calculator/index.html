Guide / Clock and calculator

The mortgage calculator

Work out a payment and what extra payments save, with a rate you type.

How do I turn it on?

In Settings ▸ Features: Calculator, turn on Loans and Converters. It is off to start. The calculator then has a choice at its top: Sums, Mortgage, Car Loan and Converters, and on the Mac they are in the View menu too. With it off, the calculator is exactly as it was.

Every rate is one you type: nothing is looked up, and no rates come with the app. What you type is forgotten when the app locks, unless you save it as a scenario.

What you enter

  • The price, and the down payment as an amount or a percent.
  • The interest rate in percent a year, and the term in years. 15 and 30 years are shown side by side.
  • Property tax a year, home insurance a year, PMI in percent a year, and HOA dues a month, if you have them.
  • Extra payments: a monthly amount, a yearly amount, which is paid with every twelfth payment, and one amount once, at a payment number.

Typed fields are read as sums, so “250,000” and “250000 × 0.2” both work, with or without a currency sign, and a rate’s “6.5%” is 6.5 percent. Each extra goes to what you owe.

What it shows

  • The monthly payment, broken down into principal and interest, tax, insurance, PMI and HOA.
  • The payoff month, the interest in all, and what the extra payments save in interest and in months.
  • PMI is paid until you owe 80% of the price, and not at all with 20% down. It shows how long PMI is paid and what it comes to.

The schedule

The schedule shows by year, with each year’s months under it, or by payment. Export Schedule saves it as a spreadsheet or a CSV file, after saying the file isn’t encrypted. On the Mac it is saved where you choose, and on the iPhone it is handed to the iPhone’s own sheet for sending or saving.

Saving a scenario

While your own budget is open, Mortgage has a part called Saved in Your Budget. Save Scenario… asks for a name, and keeps the figures you typed, as you typed them, in your budget’s encrypted file. An empty name becomes “Mortgage 1”. Choose a saved scenario to bring its figures back, and use the trash button beside it to delete it. It is not offered in the sample budget. A scenario saved by a later version of the app can’t be read by this one, and it says so.
