The apps

  • Bellwether Budget

    A household budget you keep by hand, on iPhone and Mac. Typing in each purchase takes a few seconds, and you see the money leave. It tells you what’s safe to spend and whether the bills are covered. It never connects to your bank, so there’s no bank login.

    In development

  • Small Flock

    A calm game where you’re the border collie, gathering a small flock into the stone fold. There’s no clock and no score, and you can’t lose.

    In development

  • A notes app

    A plain place to write things down. You open it and start typing, with no account to make first, and what you write is yours.

    In development

  • A reminders app

    Write down what needs doing and keep it in a few lists. Give a reminder a day and a time, and the app gives you a nudge when it’s due.

    In development

  • A household calendar

    A private calendar for a household: birthdays, appointments, and who’s away and when. You see the month at a glance and each day in order.

About Stonefold Labs

Stonefold Labs is small. We make apps for iPhone and Mac, and each one does one everyday job.

A fold is a pen with stone walls, built to keep a flock together, and that’s where our name comes from. The apps follow the same idea: what you put in one is yours. You never make an account, nothing about you is tracked, and you pay once.

This website keeps the same promises. It uses no cookies and no analytics, and it loads nothing from anyone else. The Privacy page has the details.

  • No account

    There’s nothing to sign up for. You open an app and use it.

  • No ads or tracking

    The apps show no ads, and they don’t track you or collect analytics.

  • On your devices

    If an app keeps something of yours, it’s stored on your devices, encrypted. We can’t see what’s in it.

  • Works offline

    The apps work without an internet connection.

  • Pay once

    You pay one time. There’s no subscription and there are no in-app purchases.

  • No AI

    There’s no AI in the apps. No language model runs anywhere in them.
