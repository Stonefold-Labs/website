Features

Everything in Stonefold.

Pick the parts you want when you first open Stonefold, and change them any time in Settings.

Plan your days

  • Calendar

    Your dates, by month and by day.

  • Reminders

    Lists of things to do, with alerts.

  • Clock

    Timers, alarms, a stopwatch and a world clock.

  • Countdowns

    Count the days to anything.

  • Focus timer

    Focused work, with breaks.

Write things down

  • Notes

    Plain notes. The first line is the title.

  • Journal

    One entry a day, with an optional mood.

  • Recipes

    Recipes you can cook from.

  • Logins

    Usernames and passwords, with no autofill.

  • Password generator

    Make a strong password or PIN, offline.

  • Authenticator

    Sign-in codes for two-step sign-in.

Keep track of money

  • Budget

    A budget you type by hand.

  • Calculator

    A simple calculator that keeps nothing.

  • Mortgage and car loan calculators

    Work out a payment, with rates you type.

  • Converters

    Units, dates, tips and rates.

  • Net worth

    What you own, what you owe, and the difference.

  • Debt payoff

    Compare ways to pay off what you owe.

  • Retirement planner

    Plan your years and see how long your money lasts.

Papers, habits and upkeep

  • Files

  • Habits

    Check off what you want to do regularly.

  • Home and car maintenance logs

    Keep a record of the work on your home and car.

Make it yours

  • Themes

    Eight themes and four typefaces.

  • The keyboard

    On the Mac, you rarely need the trackpad.

  • Panels and layouts

    On the Mac, arrange panels your way.

  • Extras you switch on

    Extras are off until you switch them on.

How the parts work together

Two switches in Settings let the parts share what they know. Both start off.

  • Bills on the calendar

    Each bill shows on its due day, with its amount.

  • Bills in Reminders

    Waiting bills sit at the top of Today. Choose Paid and it’s recorded in the budget.

  • Reminders on the calendar

    A reminder with a day shows under that day’s events.
