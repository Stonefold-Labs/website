Privacy

Last changed 22 November 2026.

In short

We collect nothing about you on this website. The company that hosts it keeps a security log, described below. If you write to us, we have your message. That’s all.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. We don’t even count the sheep. It has no forms, and it loads no fonts, pictures, scripts or other files from anyone else.

The theme picker at the top of the page is the one small script of our own on this site. Until you pick a theme, the page follows your device’s light or dark setting. If you pick one, your browser remembers it on your device as a single word, such as “sage”. That word is never sent to us or to anyone else, and clearing this site’s data forgets it. Headings, and the name Stonefold wherever it is written, are set in a small font file of our own, served from this site like the stylesheet. Everything else uses fonts that come with your device.

This site’s earlier address, stonefoldlabs.com, sends you on to this one. That old address is answered by Cloudflare, which does nothing but pass you here. Like every web server, Cloudflare receives your IP address when your browser asks, and we don’t see it.

The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web server, GitHub’s receives your IP address when your browser asks for a page, and GitHub says it logs and stores that address for security purposes. We don’t see those logs and have no way to get them. GitHub’s privacy statement covers what GitHub does with them.

If you write to us

When you send an e-mail to support@stonefoldlabs.com, we receive your address and whatever you write. It is kept in our mailbox, which Proton Mail provides, and used only to answer you. We don’t add you to any list. Ask, and we’ll delete your messages.

The app

Stonefold has no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it works offline. What you keep is stored on your devices, encrypted. How it works says how.

The app uses the internet only for iCloud sync, and only if you turn it on. With sync on, each change is encrypted on your device with a key only your iPhone and Mac hold, then sent through your own iCloud. We run no servers and receive nothing. Apple can see that your Apple Account is syncing, and when.

In Settings, What Leaves This Device says in plain sentences what stays on your device and what goes anywhere.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
