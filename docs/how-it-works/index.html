How it works

Stonefold lives on your devices.

What you keep stays on your iPhone and Mac, encrypted, and the app works without the internet. If you want your devices to match, you can turn on iCloud sync, which stays end-to-end encrypted. There’s no account, no ads and no tracking.

  • Works offline
  • Encrypted on your device
  • iCloud sync, if you want it
  • Take it with you
  • No account, ads or tracking

1It works without the internet.

Everything you add to Stonefold is saved on your device first. There’s nothing to sign in to and nothing to wait for, so the app opens, searches and saves the same on a plane as it does at home.

  • Turn on airplane mode and nothing changes.
  • Alerts for reminders, events, alarms and timers are set on your device and ring from it.
  • The only thing in Stonefold that uses the internet is iCloud sync, and only if you turn it on.

2Everything is encrypted on your device.

What you keep is stored in encrypted files on your device, and the keys that open them stay on your devices. We run no server for the app, so we can’t see what’s in it.

  • Encrypted, file by file

    Everything you put in is stored in encrypted files. The calendar and reminders, the notes, the budget and Files each have a file of their own and their own random key.

  • Keys stay on your devices

    Each key is made on your device and kept on your devices. We never see one, and a key is never sent to us.

  • One unlock

    Face ID, Touch ID or your device’s passcode opens everything. By default, anyone who can unlock your device can open the app. You can lock the budget or the notes with a passphrase of your own.

  • Kept out of logs and Spotlight

    Your words and figures aren’t written to logs, caches or Spotlight. The one exception is the words in an alert, which you can switch off in Settings.

  • The recovery sheet

    One page you print, with a code for each file, is the way back in if a device’s keys are lost. Lose every device and the sheet, and nobody can open what you kept, including us.

  • A page that tells you

    In Settings, What Leaves This Device says in plain sentences what stays on your device, what goes anywhere only because you chose it, and what Apple can see.

3Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on iCloud sync.

Out of the box, nothing leaves your device. Sync is a switch in Settings, and it starts off.

When you turn it on, each change is sealed on your device first, with a key only your devices hold. It then travels through your own iCloud to your other devices, and each one opens it with the same key. Sync is end-to-end encrypted. Only your devices hold the key. Sync runs through your own iCloud, and we run no servers.

  • Every change is sealed before it leaves your device.
  • A new device gets the key from one of your devices, with a code you scan or type.
  • Apple can see that your Apple Account is syncing, and when, because sync runs through iCloud.

The price of this privacy is said plainly when you turn sync on: if you lose every device and every copy of the key, nobody can open what you kept, and that includes us. Print the recovery sheet and keep it where you would keep a key.

Stays on your device

  • What you keep, in encrypted files
  • The keys that open them
  • Your settings
  • Your alerts, which are set on your device and ring from it

Can leave, only because you chose it

  • Sealed changes, through your own iCloud, if you turn sync on
  • An export, in a folder you choose (it isn’t encrypted, so Stonefold asks first)
  • A saved copy, sealed with a passphrase you set, wherever you save it
  • The recovery sheet, a page you print

4You can export what you keep.

Your events, reminders, notes and budget can be exported in formats other apps open. Export Everything, in Settings, writes them all into one new folder.

  • Calendar.ics holds every event, for calendar apps.
  • Reminders.csv holds every reminder, for spreadsheet apps.
  • Notes is a folder with a plain text file for each note.
  • Budget.xlsx holds your transactions, accounts, categories and bills, with a summary of each month.
  • Files holds your documents exactly as they are, in the same folders.

Nothing in that folder is encrypted, so Stonefold asks first. If you choose a folder in iCloud Drive, it’s uploaded there too.

Stonefold also keeps encrypted backups of each file on your device, and Save a Copy seals a copy with a passphrase you choose, wherever you want to keep it.

5No account, no ads, no tracking.

  • No account

    There’s no sign-up and nothing to log in to. Face ID, Touch ID or your device’s passcode opens the app, and that’s all.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting code and no outside services of any kind. We run no server for the app, so we can’t see what’s in it.

  • No AI

    What Stonefold works out is arithmetic or a plain rule, and it can show how it got its answer.

  • Pay once

    Stonefold costs $24.99, paid once, for iPhone and Mac together. There’s no subscription and there are no in-app purchases.

Questions

Can you see what I keep in Stonefold?

No. We run no server for the app, so we never receive what you keep, and we can’t see what’s in it.

Do I have to turn on sync?

No. Sync is off to start, and every part of Stonefold works without it. Turn it on only if you want your devices to match.

What if I lose my iPhone?

If you use iCloud sync, your other devices still hold what you kept. Either way, your recovery sheet or a saved copy opens what you kept on a new device. If you lose every device and every copy of the key, nobody can open it, and that includes us.

What if I stop using Stonefold?

Export Everything puts your events, reminders, notes and budget in one folder, in formats other apps open, so you can carry on somewhere else.

Does anyone else get my data?

No. There’s no account, no analytics and no outside service. With sync on, Apple carries sealed changes between your devices, and it can see that your Apple Account is syncing and when.
