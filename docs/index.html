About Stonefold Labs

Stonefold Labs makes apps for iPhone and Mac. Each one does a single job, and does it plainly.

A fold is a pen with stone walls, built to keep a flock together. The apps are made the same way: what you put in one is yours. There is no account to make, nothing is tracked, and you pay once.

This website keeps the same promises: no cookies, no analytics, and nothing loaded from anyone else. Privacy has the details.

  • No account

    No sign-up.

  • No ads or tracking

    No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

  • On your devices

    In the apps that keep something of yours, it is stored on your devices, encrypted. We can’t see what’s in it.

  • Works offline

    Works without the internet.

  • Pay once

    No subscription, no in-app purchases.

  • No AI

    No language model, anywhere.
