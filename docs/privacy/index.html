Privacy

Last changed 18 November 2026.

In short

We collect nothing about you on this website. The company that hosts it keeps a security log, described below. If you write to us, we have your message. That’s all.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It has no forms, and it loads no fonts, pictures, scripts or other files from anyone else.

The theme picker at the top of the page is one small script of our own. It lets you wear any of the eight themes the Stonefold app has, such as Sage or Linen. Until you pick one, the page follows your device’s light or dark setting. If you do pick one, your browser remembers the choice on your device, as the single word for the theme, such as “sage” or “linen”. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and clearing this site’s data in your browser forgets it. The picker uses only the fonts that come with your device and loads nothing from anyone else.

This site’s earlier address, stonefoldlabs.com, sends you on to this one. That old address is answered by Cloudflare, which does nothing but pass you here. Like every web server, Cloudflare receives your IP address when your browser asks, and we don’t see it.

The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web server, GitHub’s receives your IP address when your browser asks for a page, and GitHub says it logs and stores that address for security purposes. We don’t see those logs and have no way to get them. GitHub’s privacy statement covers what GitHub does with them.

If you write to us

When you send an e-mail to support@stonefoldlabs.com, we receive your address and whatever you write. It is kept in our mailbox, which Proton Mail provides, and used only to answer you. We don’t add you to any list. Ask, and we’ll delete your messages.

The app

Stonefold isn’t released yet. When it is, its privacy policy will be on this page and inside the app.

It follows one rule throughout: no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The home page says how what you keep in it is encrypted.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
