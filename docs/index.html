A private app for everything.

Stonefold brings your calendar, reminders, notes, budget, tools, and files together in one app. It runs locally on your iPhone and Mac, works offline, and keeps your data encrypted. There’s no AI, account, ads, or tracking. iCloud sync is optional, and your data stays in files you own.
