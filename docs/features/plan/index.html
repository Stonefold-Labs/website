Plan your days

Your calendar, reminders and clocks, kept on your devices.

See how it works All features

Dates, to-dos and time

Extras on this page are off until you turn them on in Settings.

Calendar

Your dates, by month and by day.

  • Month and day

    See the busy days, then open one.

  • Plain events

    A title, a place and notes, all day or at set times.

  • Extras

    Repeats, alerts, more calendars and time zones.

Reminders

Lists of things to do, with alerts.

  • Today

    What’s due or overdue, from every list.

  • Repeats

    A repeating reminder moves on when you mark it done.

  • Done and Snooze

    Both are on the alert, so you needn’t open the app.

Clock

Run several timers at once, set alarms that repeat, time something with laps, or check the time in another city.

  • Timers and alarms

    Pick a length or set your own. Alarms can repeat on the days you choose.

  • Stopwatch and world clock

    See your quickest lap. Cities come from your device, so nothing is looked up online.

  • Countdowns

    Give a countdown a name and a date, and Stonefold tells you how long is left. It can repeat every year, like a birthday.

    • In plain words. “In 12 days”, then days and hours when it’s close.
    • After the day. It moves to the bottom and counts up. Nothing is deleted for you.

  • Focus timer

    Work for 25 minutes, rest for 5, and take a longer break after four rounds. You can change every length.

    • Pause or skip a break. It keeps time even when the app is closed.
    • Plain counts. It tells you how many sessions you finished today.

  • Boards

    Turn on Boards and view any reminders list as cards in columns: To Do, Doing and Done to start. Drag a card to move it. A card moved into Done is marked done.

    • Your own columns. Rename, move or delete a column, or add one before Done.
