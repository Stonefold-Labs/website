A budget, and the tools around it

Extras on this page are off until you turn them on in Settings.

Budget

A budget you type by hand. It never connects to a bank.

  • Where you stand

    What’s safe to spend, whether bills are covered, and whether you’re on pace.

  • Can I afford it

    Type an amount and find out.

  • A spreadsheet to keep

    Export your budget as a working spreadsheet, formulas included.

Retirement planner

Type in your savings, your ages and what you expect to spend, then change any of them to see what happens. Like everything Stonefold works out, it’s plain arithmetic, with no AI.

  • Kept on your device

    Everything is typed by hand and stored encrypted.

  • Change one thing

    Move an age or an amount and watch the rest follow.

  • Calculator

    Type a sum and press Return. Decimals work the way you write them, so 0.1 plus 0.2 is 0.3. On the Mac, ⌃⌘C opens it. On the iPhone, it’s under More.

    • Turn on Loans and Converters in Settings for mortgages, car loans and conversions.

  • Mortgage and car loan calculators

    You type the rate yourself, so there’s nothing to look up online. Turn on Loans and Converters in Settings to find them in the Calculator.

    • Mortgage. Price, down payment, rate and term, plus tax, insurance and HOA. See 15 and 30 years side by side, and what extra payments save.
    • Car loan. Compare 36, 48, 60 and 72 months, with trade-in, sales tax and fees.
    • The schedule. See it by year. Export it as a CSV file or spreadsheet. Stonefold asks first, because the file isn’t encrypted.

  • Converters

    Find them in the Calculator, with Loans and Converters turned on. Nothing is fetched.

    • Units. Length, weight, volume, temperature, area, speed and cooking measures.
    • Dates. Days between two dates, or the date a number of days away.
    • Tips and rates. Split a bill, or convert an amount at a rate you type. No rates come with the app.

  • Net worth

    Turn on Net Worth in the Budget to see your accounts, the totals and a chart over time. You type everything, so Stonefold never connects to a bank.

    • Things outside the budget. Add something you own, and update its value when it changes.

  • Debt payoff

    List each card or loan with its balance, rate and minimum, add an extra amount each month, and see how long it takes.

    • Three orders, side by side. Smallest balance first, highest rate first, or your own. Each shows its payoff month and total interest.
    • Month by month. See the schedule for the order you pick.
