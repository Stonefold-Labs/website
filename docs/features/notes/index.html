Features

Write things down

Notes for anything, plus kinds of note for a journal, recipes, logins, a family binder and sign-in codes.

See how it works All features

Notes, and the kinds of note you keep most

Extras on this page are off until you turn them on in Settings.

Notes

Plain notes. Start typing, and the first line is the title.

  • Search

    Finds any word in any note as you type.

  • Pinned, then recent

    Pinned notes first, then the ones you changed last.

  • Links open only when you ask

    No previews, no pictures from the web.

Kinds of note

Pick the kind when you make a note. A kind is still a note: it’s encrypted the same way and opens with the same unlock.

  • Journal

    Write about today, or open an earlier day. Your journal is encrypted like the rest of your notes.

    • A mood, if you like. Hard, Low, Okay, Good or Great.
    • Quick to open. On the Mac, press ⌃⌘J for today’s entry.

  • Recipes

    Keep servings, times, ingredients and steps together, and find a recipe by searching any of them.

    • Change the servings. Press plus or minus and every amount adjusts.

  • Logins

    Keep a site, a username and a password together. Stonefold has no autofill and no browser extension, and it never goes online.

    • Hidden until you ask. Passwords show as dots. Show reveals one for 30 seconds. The first time after you unlock, Stonefold asks you to confirm it’s you.
    • Copy for this device only. What you copy leaves the clipboard after 90 seconds.
    • Said plainly. By default, anyone who can unlock your device can open your logins. A passphrase on your notes adds a lock of its own.
    • Exports. Passwords stay out of exports unless you choose to include them, which asks first that this is your device.

  • Binder

    Fill in contacts, doctors, insurance, accounts and where the papers are, and leave the rest empty. Make a PDF to print.

    • Numbers stay hidden. An account or policy number shows as dots until you show this is your device.
    • Never a password. Online accounts are named by site only. Passwords stay in your Logins notes.

  • Password generator

    Make a password of 8 to 64 characters, a PIN, or three groups of letters and numbers. It tells you how strong each one is.

    • Where to find it. Inside Add Login, or on its own. On the Mac, ⌃⌘G opens it.

  • Authenticator

    Add the key a site gives you, and Stonefold shows the code as it changes. Tap to copy.

    • Codes stay hidden until you confirm it’s you, once each time you unlock.
    • A copied code stays on this device and leaves the clipboard after a minute.
