Features

Everything in Stonefold.

One app for iPhone and Mac, with one unlock. Choose the parts you want when you first open it, and change your mind in Settings any time.

See how it works How it stays private

Plan your days

  • Calendar

    Your dates, by the month and by the day, with today circled.

  • Reminders

    Lists of things to do, with alerts.

  • Clock

    Timers, alarms, a stopwatch with laps and a world clock.

  • Countdowns

    Name a day and see how long is left, in days, and in hours when it’s close.

  • Focus timer

    Work in focused stretches, with short breaks between them.

Write things down

  • Notes

    Plain notes. Start typing, and the first line is the title.

  • Journal

    One entry for each day, with an optional mood.

  • Recipes

    Recipes you can cook from, with amounts that scale.

  • Logins

    Usernames and passwords, kept encrypted, with no autofill.

  • Password generator

    Make a strong password, or a PIN, without the internet.

  • Authenticator

    The codes for two-step sign-in, kept with your notes.

Keep track of money

  • Budget

    A budget you type by hand. It never connects to a bank.

  • Calculator

    A small calculator that keeps nothing of yours.

  • Mortgage and car loan calculators

    Work out a payment and its interest, with rates you type.

  • Converters

    Convert units, count days between dates, split a tip, or work out an amount at a rate you type.

  • Net worth

    See what you own and what you owe, with a chart over time.

  • Debt payoff

    See which order pays off what you owe soonest, and how much interest it costs.

  • Retirement planner

    Try your savings and ages and see how the plan holds up.

Papers, habits and upkeep

  • Files

    Your documents and warranties, kept encrypted, with scanning on the iPhone.

  • Habits

    Check off what you want to do regularly, and look back at how it’s going.

  • Home and car maintenance logs

    Write down the work done to your home and your car, and see what’s due next.

Make it yours

  • Themes

    Eight themes and four typefaces, worn by the whole app.

  • The keyboard

    On the Mac, the trackpad is rarely needed.

  • Panels and layouts

    On the Mac, dock panels where you want them and save the layout.

  • Features you switch on

    Each section’s extras are switches in Settings, off to start.
