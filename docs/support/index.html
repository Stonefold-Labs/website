Help in the app

Stonefold has its own Help, with a search box. On the Mac, choose Help, then Stonefold Help, or press ⌘?. On the iPhone, open Settings and choose Help.

Questions

I have a new iPhone or Mac.

Turn on iCloud sync on both. Or use Save a Copy on the old device and Open a Copy on the new one, with the passphrase you chose. Your recovery sheet also opens what it covers on a new device.

I forgot a passphrase.

Your printed recovery sheet is the way back in. Without it, nobody can open the budget or notes you locked, and we can’t either.

How do I take my things out?

In Settings, choose Export Everything. It writes your events, reminders, notes and budget into one new folder. Nothing in it is encrypted, so Stonefold asks first.

Can you see what I keep?

No. We run no server for the app.
