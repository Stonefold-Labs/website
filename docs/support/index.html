Support

Write to support@stonefoldlabs.com. We read every message, and we answer within two working days.

Please don’t send us what you keep in the app, such as your budget or your notes. We never need it to help you.

Help in the app

Stonefold has its own Help, with a page for each part of the app and a search box. On the Mac, choose Help, then Stonefold Help, or press ⌘?. On the iPhone, open Settings and choose Help.

Questions

I have a new iPhone or Mac. How do I bring my things?

Turn on iCloud sync on both devices. Or use Save a Copy on the old device and Open a Copy on the new one: a copy is sealed with a passphrase you choose, so keep that passphrase safe. Your recovery sheet also opens what it covers on a new device.

I forgot the passphrase I set on my budget or my notes.

The recovery sheet you printed is the way back in. Without it, nobody can open them, and we have no way to open them for you.

How do I take my things out of Stonefold?

In Settings, choose Export Everything. It writes your events, reminders, notes and budget into one new folder, in formats other apps open. Nothing in that folder is encrypted, so Stonefold asks first.

Can you see what I keep in the app?

No. We run no server for the app, so we never receive what you keep, and we can’t see what’s in it.
