How it works.

Your things stay on your iPhone and Mac, encrypted, and the app works offline. If you want your iPhone and Mac to match, you can turn on iCloud sync.

  1. 1It works offline.

    Everything you add is saved on your device first. There’s nothing to sign in to and nothing to wait for.

    Alerts are set on your device and ring from it. Only iCloud sync uses the internet, and only if you turn it on.

  2. 2Everything is encrypted on your device.

    Your things are stored in encrypted files, and the keys stay on your iPhone and Mac. We run no server for the app, so we can’t see what’s in it.

    Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode opens the app. By default, anyone who can unlock your device can open it. You can lock the budget or the notes with a passphrase of your own.

    The recovery sheet, a page you print, is the way back in if a device’s keys are lost. If you lose your iPhone and Mac and the sheet, nobody can open what you kept, including us.

  3. 3Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on iCloud sync.

    Out of the box, nothing leaves your device. Sync is a switch in Settings, and it starts off.

    Turn it on and each change is sealed on your device first, with a key only your iPhone and Mac hold. It then travels through your own iCloud to your other iPhone or Mac. Sync is end-to-end encrypted. Only your iPhone and Mac hold the key. It runs through your own iCloud, and we run no servers.

    Apple can see that your Apple Account is syncing, and when, because sync runs through iCloud.

  4. 4Take your data out any time.

    Your data belongs to you. Export Everything, in Settings, writes what you keep into a folder you choose, whenever you want. It comes out in everyday, open formats that other apps read easily, so you are never locked into Stonefold, and we hold no copy of what you keep.

    • .icsCalendar one file any calendar app opens
    • .csvReminders a row for each, for spreadsheet apps
    • .md or .txtNotes a plain text file for each note
    • .txtFlashcards plain text that Anki brings in
    • .xlsxBudget one workbook for spreadsheet apps
    • as they areFiles every document, in the same folders

    Nothing in that folder is encrypted, so Stonefold asks first.

  5. 5No account, no ads, no tracking, no AI.

    No sign-up, and nothing to log in to. No ads, no analytics, no outside services, and we can’t see what’s in the app. Anything Stonefold works out is plain arithmetic, and it can show how.
