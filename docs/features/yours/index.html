Look, keys and layout

Themes

Pick a theme and the whole app wears it: Sage, Linen, Night, Ember, Paper, Dusk, Clay or Plain, which follows your device.

  • Typefaces

    System, Rounded, Serif or Monospace.

  • Density

    Compact, Standard or Roomy.

  • An icon to match

    On the Mac, the app icon starts out matching your theme.

The keyboard

Press ⌘K, type a few letters, and go anywhere or do anything.

  • The command box

    Finds menu items, sections, notes, lists and settings.

  • Two-key jumps

    Press G, then a letter, to go to a section.

  • Quick add

    Type “dentist fri 3pm” and add it as an event, a reminder or a note.

  • See the keys

    Hold ⌘ for a moment to see the keys for where you are.

  • Panels and layouts

    Put Events, Reminders, Notes and Money side by side. Dock them left, right or along the bottom, stack them in tabs, and save the layout.

  • Extras you switch on

    Each part has a small core that everyone gets. Anything beyond it is a switch in Settings, off to start, so Stonefold stays simple until you want more.
